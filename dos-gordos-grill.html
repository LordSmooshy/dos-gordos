Our Story

Born from Two
Cultures, One Dream

We grew up in El Paso, Texas and Trujillo, Peru — two places where food isn't just sustenance, it's memory, identity, and love. When we brought our family to Laveen, Arizona, we brought every recipe, every tradition, and every grandmother's secret with us.

Dos Gordos Grill isn't a chain. It isn't a concept. It's our home — and when you walk through our door, you're a guest at our table. Everything is made from scratch, every single day.
